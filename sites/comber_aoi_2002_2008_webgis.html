2 · 2002 vs 2008 change (computed, MODIS 250 m)

For each pixel we computed absolute luminance difference between the years, removed the scene-wide median offset (atmosphere/season), and stretched the local difference. Blocks of 16×16 px were ranked. At 250 m these are regional land-cover / shoreline change hotspots, not an AOI-scale or object-level result. The strongest 2002→2008 change sits south of the site (toward Belfast / Belfast Lough) — consistent with urban / intertidal change, not a discrete object.

4 · Why MODIS, not Landsat here

  • Landsat 30 m is the ideal AOI scale, but the only open access (Planetary Computer) silently ignores the crop bbox, and GIBS/WELD has no global 2002–2008 natural-colour layer. Without GDAL/COG tooling in this environment, a correctly-cropped Landsat crop could not be produced or verified.
  • MODIS 250 m is the only correctly-georeferenced 2002–2008 source retrievable, so the historical comparison is shown at regional scale. It is valid for landscape/shoreline change, not for sub-metre objects.
  • For AOI-scale (sub-metre) 2002–2008 history, only commercial air photography works: OSNI / Land & Property Services (authoritative for NI), Bluesky, Getmapping, NCAP — order-only, not downloadable here.
  • No visual verification by the agent (it cannot view images); change layers are computed from decoded pixels and should be confirmed by a human.
