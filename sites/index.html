3 · Interpretation status

At the available verified resolutions, this page makes no defensible claim of a discrete ground disturbance at the point. Tidal water, marsh vegetation, cloud, haze, and mixed pixels dominate this coastal AOI. The former Landsat pixel-difference hotspots have been withdrawn because their georeferencing was invalid.

4 · Data and limitations

  • MODIS is approximately 250 m; MERIS is coarse historical optical imagery. A drum-scale object is sub-pixel.
  • The earlier Landsat scenes remain source evidence in the repository but are not mapped here until downloaded as true georeferenced rasters and cropped using their GeoTIFF geotransforms.
  • Commercial OSNI/LPS or other licensed aerial photography is required for ~0.25–1 m 2002–2008 review.
  • No conclusion about burial, concealment, or criminal intent is made.
