2 · Layers & how they are georeferenced

  • Esri World Imagery — live tile layer (Web Mercator), the high-resolution base you see the area in.
  • MODIS Terra 2002 / 2005 / 2008 — NASA GIBS WMS, requested with an explicit Comber bounding box, so each image is placed at its true coordinates (250 m). Toggle against the basemap to compare dated land/water state.
  • Esri Wayback 2014 & 2024 — historical 0.6 m aerial tiles fetched by their exact Web Mercator tile addresses, mosaicked and placed at true coordinates. Use these for the clearest before/after of the area (note: 2014 is post-event, not 2002–2008).

Earlier a Planetary Computer "preview" was used that silently ignored the crop box and returned full ~280 km scenes; those mis-placed layers have been removed. This version uses only sources whose coordinates are verified.

3 · Limitations

  • Resolution. MODIS 250 m cannot resolve a drum; Wayback 0.6 m is the clearest free view but is 2014–2024 (post-event).
  • Drum-scale 2002–2008 = commercial. Only ~0.25–1 m coverage of that window is order-only air photography (OSNI/LPS, Bluesky, Getmapping, NCAP). Not freely downloadable.
  • No determination. Nothing here infers burial, concealment, or intent.
