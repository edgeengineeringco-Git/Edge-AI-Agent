3 - Change Interpretation (2002 to 2008)

Method. The 2002 and 2008 AOI crops are georeferenced to the identical geographic bounding box, so they are pixel-co-registered. For each pixel we computed absolute luminance difference |L2002 - L2008|, removed the scene-wide median offset (to cancel uniform sensor / atmosphere / seasonal tone differences), and stretched the remaining local difference to produce the change maps above. Blocks of 16x16 px were ranked by mean local difference; the top blocks are listed with their coordinates. This is a coarse (30 m) land-cover / shoreline change screen.

6 - Limitations and what is still required

  • Resolution. All imagery here is satellite (Landsat 30 m; MODIS 250 m). A buried drum (~1-2 m) is invisible at this scale. The change maps show large-area change only.
  • Drum-scale data is commercial. The only ~0.25-1 m coverage of 2002-2008 is order-only air photography: OSNI / Land and Property Services (authoritative for NI - start here), Bluesky (oldaerialphotos.com), Getmapping, NCAP. None is freely downloadable.
  • No visual verification. The agent cannot view images; the change maps are computed from decoded pixel values and must be confirmed by a human.
  • No determination. Nothing here infers burial, concealment, or intent. Coordinates are change hotspots for review, not conclusions.
