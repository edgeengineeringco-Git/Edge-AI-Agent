Methodology & Dose Equations

Every conversion constant is traceable to UNSCEAR 2024, ICRP 137, or EU BSS 2013/59/Euratom. No magic numbers in the UI.

Tool scope: External terrestrial (0.4) + radon/thoron inhalation (1.8) = 2.2 mSv/yr — excludes ingestion & cosmic.

External Gamma Dose Rate

Dair = 0.462·ARa + 0.604·ATh + 0.041·AK

Absorbed dose rate in air (nGy/h) from activity concentrations (Bq/kg). Coefficients are UNSCEAR standard conversion factors.

UNSCEAR 2024; A_Ra = U-238 chain, A_Th = Th-232 chain, A_K = K-40

Annual External Dose

Eext = Dair × 8760 × OF × 0.7 × 10⁻⁶

8760 hours/year; OF = occupancy factor (0.2 outdoor + 0.8 indoor); 0.7 Sv/Gy conversion; ×10⁻⁶ to convert nGy→mSv.

UNSCEAR 2024 methodology

Activity Concentration → Bq/kg

AU = eU(ppm) × 12.22
ATh = eTh(ppm) × 4.06
AK = K(%) × 313

Conversion from radiometric survey units (ppm eU, ppm eTh, %K) to activity concentrations (Bq/kg).

IAEA; UNSCEAR conversion constants

Geogenic Radon Potential (GRP)

GRP = f(ARa226, permeability, fault_dist, lineament_density, lithology_factor)

Radon source term (Ra-226) modulated by soil permeability, proximity to faults, lineament density, and lithology-specific emanation factor.

EU radon potential models; USGS geogenic RP

Global Thoron Potential (GTP)

ATh232 → emanation(0.1–0.3)
→ indoor gas → EEC(×0.02)
→ dose (×40 nSv × 7000h)

Thoron estimated geogenically from Th-232 (globally available via geochemistry + eTh radiometry). Calibrated so world-average soil yields ~0.1 mSv/yr.

UNSCEAR 2024; RadoNorm thoron methodology

Radium Equivalent Activity

Raeq = ARa + 1.43·ATh + 0.077·AK

Weighted sum assigning hazard equivalence to a single radium activity. Used for construction material safety (EU BSS activity index).

OECD/NEA; EU BSS 2013/59/Euratom

Radon → Effective Dose

ERn = CRn × F × t × DCF

C_Rn = indoor concentration (Bq/m³); F = equilibrium factor (0.4 default); t = annual exposure hours; DCF = dose conversion factor per ICRP 137.

ICRP 137 (3 mSv per mJ·h·m⁻³); EU BSS: 300 Bq/m³ → ~10 mSv/yr

EU BSS Activity Index

I = ARa/300 + ATh/200 + AK/3000

Activity concentration index for building materials. I ≥ 1 triggers dose assessment under EU BSS 2013/59/Euratom.

EU BSS 2013/59/Euratom, Annex VIII

Core Architecture

6-Layer Processing Pipeline

From raw geological data to paper-ready provenance — geology is the primary predictor at every stage.

1

INGEST

Per bounding box: OneGeology lithology (PRIMARY), Sentinel-2 composites, GLO-30 DEM, GEM faults, SoilGrids, geochemical atlas U/Th/K₂O, JRC Atlas, airborne radiometric. Cache to PostGIS with provenance.

Open data · licence-logged
2

FEATURE EXTRACTION

Lithology class per polygon (OneGeology ground truth). K/U/Th activities: PRIMARY via lithology→activity lookup table; OVERRIDE via airborne radiometric / geochemical atlas. Lineament density, fault distance, soil permeability, elevation, latitude.

Geology-primary
3

DOSE MODELS

External gamma (UNSCEAR coefficients), Geogenic Radon Potential (GRP), Global Thoron Potential (GTP). Measured C_Rn / C_Tn override geogenic estimates where available. All via dose_calculation_core.py v4 — no re-implementation.

dose_calculation_core.py
4

RISK CLASSIFICATION

Traffic-light tiers: annual terrestrial dose, indoor radon, gamma dose rate, radium equivalent. Flags: WHO radon (100 Bq/m³), UNSCEAR upper-normal (1500 nGy/h), EU BSS activity index I ≥ 1.

UNSCEAR 2024 base
5

VISUALISATION

VisQuill-style dose fingerprint triangle per polygon (radon / thoron / gamma arms). Colour by dominant source. Risk traffic-light overlay. Timeline slider (seasonal/annual). MapLibre GL JS base map. Provenance panel with per-parameter confidence.

MapLibre GL JS
6

AGENT LOOP

Coverage-gap detection ("no airborne eTh for region X → using lithology prior for Th/thoron"). Confidence scoring per polygon. Provenance logging for paper-readiness. Threshold alerts.

Paper-ready provenance

Standards & References

Every number traceable to cited data sources and international standards.

📖 UNSCEAR 2024

United Nations Scientific Committee on the Effects of Atomic Radiation — latest global report. Total natural background ≈ 3.0 mSv/yr (up from 2.4 due to improved data/methodology). Based on ~2,000 publications and data from 61 member states including national radon AND thoron surveys.

UNSCEAR 2024 Report to the General Assembly

📖 ICRP 137

International Commission on Radiological Protection Publication 137. Radon dose conversion: 3 mSv per mJ·h·m⁻³. Establishes the framework for occupational and public radon dose assessment.

ICRP Publication 137 (2017)

📖 EU BSS 2013/59/Euratom

European Basic Safety Standards Directive. Radon reference level: 300 Bq/m³ (indoor) → ~10 mSv/yr. Activity index I for building materials. Establishes legal framework for radiation protection in EU member states.

Council Directive 2013/59/Euratom

📖 WHO Radon Handbook

World Health Organization Handbook on Indoor Radon. Recommends national reference level of 100 Bq/m³ (or 300 where not feasible). Public health guidance for radon risk reduction.

WHO Handbook on Indoor Radon (2009)
