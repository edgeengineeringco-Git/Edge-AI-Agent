Ground-disturbance evidence & limitations

No validated ground-disturbance or burial signature is presented here.

Honest position on what the available free data can and cannot support:

MODIS / MERIS (2002–2008)
≈250 m / coarse. Regional context only. Cannot locate a 1–2 m object.
Landsat 5/7 (2002–2008)
~30 m. Can show large disturbances; not drum-scale. Properly georeferenced 30 m crops can be generated on request.
Esri Wayback (2014–2024)
0.3–0.6 m, but post-event. For human inspection of later disturbance only.
Commercial 2002–2008
Sub-metre (IKONOS/QuickBird/SPOT/GeoEye/ALOS) and OSNI/LPS air photos: required for true drum-scale evidence. See section 5.

No determination of burial, concealment, or intent is made. Any candidate feature must be confirmed by a human or image-capable system using licensed high-res imagery.
