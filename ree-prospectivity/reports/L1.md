# L1 — Tellus airborne Equivalent Thorium (eTh)

**Status: CONFIRMED** (2026-09-13)

**Source.** GSI Tellus airborne radiometric grid, EPSG:2157 (ITM). The native 50 m
GSI Geophysics REST service (`.../IE_GSI_GSNI_Radiometric_Equivalent_Thorium_50m_IE32_ITM_GRID/MapServer`)
was probed exhaustively: `export` returns a rendered PNG, `identify` returns the
colormap RGB (not the value), WMS `GetFeatureInfo` is disabled, and no ImageServer
variant exists. GDAL/WCS could not be installed (non-root container, PEP 668). Therefore
exact 50 m eTh ppm could not be pulled from the live service this session. The verified
raw eTh band (band 3 of K/eU/eTh/dose) was taken from the project's existing resampled
grid (`edge-ai-agent-site/data/radiometrics/values.bin`, 630×495, ITM-bounded) — this is
**genuine GSI Tellus data**, not modelled or fabricated.

**INSPECT.** 311,850 cells; 153,576 valid land cells; 158,274 NaN = sea inside the
rectangular lon/lat bbox (~51%). eTh 0.003–14.941 ppm, mean 4.10, median 3.91, p99 10.18.
5 city samples: Galway 2.314, Cork 4.885, Donegal 5.931, Limerick 4.776 ppm;
Dublin sample landed on a sea/edge cell (null).

**VERIFY gates — PASS.** CRS = ITM(2157); value range within 0–50 ppm; NaNs confined to
sea (land fully populated, no block gaps); full-island land coverage.

**CROSS.** None (first layer). National eTh histogram saved to `data_ref/L1_eTh_stats.json`
for the L6 (sediment Th) cross-check.

**OPEN GAP (documented, not hidden).** Native-50 m extraction is pending GDAL/WCS (root) or
an alternate mirror; must be resolved before the Stage-2 50 m resample gate (F2). The
630×495 product is sufficient for L1–L3 confirmation and for the L2 eU/eTh correlation
check, but cannot be upsampled to 50 m.
