## 2. Intended Purpose

[App Name] is a software application intended for monitoring glucose fluctuations and providing personalised lifestyle insights to support weight management.

The app processes glucose data (entered manually or imported from compatible continuous glucose monitors) and generates personalised recommendations regarding diet, exercise, and sleep hygiene based on individual glucose response patterns.

**This app is intended to be used as an adjunct to, not a replacement for, professional medical advice and care.**

---

## 3. Intended Users

### 3.1 Primary Users
- Adults aged 18 years and older
- Individuals with prediabetes
- Individuals with Type 2 diabetes
- Individuals with metabolic syndrome seeking weight management support

### 3.2 Healthcare Provider Users
- The app may be used in consultation with healthcare providers who wish to review patient glucose trends and lifestyle adherence
- Healthcare providers do not require the app for their practice; it is a patient-facing tool

### 3.3 User Competencies
Users should be able to:
- Operate a smartphone (iOS or Android)
- Read and understand basic health information
- Follow written instructions in the app's supported languages
- Recognise when professional medical advice is needed

## 4. Indications

The app is indicated for:
- Self-monitoring of glucose fluctuations over time
- Identification of glucose patterns related to meals, exercise, and sleep
- Receipt of personalised lifestyle recommendations to support weight management
- Enhanced awareness of individual glycaemic responses to lifestyle factors
- Data sharing with healthcare providers to support clinical consultations

## 5. Contraindications

### 5.1 Absolute Contraindications
**DO NOT USE this app if:**
- You are under 18 years of age
- You have Type 1 diabetes and are NOT under direct supervision of a healthcare provider who has approved your use of this app
- You are seeking insulin dosing recommendations or emergency medical advice
- You are experiencing a medical emergency

### 5.2 Relative Contraindications
**Use with caution and only under healthcare provider guidance if:**
- You are pregnant or planning pregnancy
- You have significant renal impairment
- You have a history of severe hypoglycaemia unawareness
- You are taking medications that significantly affect glucose metabolism (e.g., systemic corticosteroids)

## 6. Warnings and Precautions

### ⚠️ CRITICAL WARNINGS

**WARNING 1: Not a Diagnostic Tool**
This app does NOT diagnose diabetes, prediabetes, or any medical condition. It does NOT replace blood tests, HbA1c measurements, or clinical assessment by a qualified healthcare professional. Always consult your doctor for diagnosis and treatment decisions.

**WARNING 2: Not for Insulin Dosing**
This app does NOT provide insulin dosing calculations, recommendations, or reminders. NEVER use this app to make decisions about insulin dosage. Insulin dosing must always be determined by your healthcare provider or according to your prescribed insulin regimen.

**WARNING 3: Not for Emergency Use**
This app is NOT designed for emergency medical situations. If you experience severe hypoglycaemia (confusion, seizures, loss of consciousness) or diabetic ketoacidosis symptoms (excessive thirst, frequent urination, nausea, abdominal pain, fruity breath), seek immediate emergency medical attention. Call your local emergency number.

**WARNING 4: Consult Your Healthcare Provider**
Always consult your healthcare provider before making significant changes to your diet, exercise routine, or medication regimen based on this app's recommendations. The app's insights are intended to support, not replace, professional medical guidance.

**WARNING 5: Data Accuracy**
The accuracy of this app's insights depends on the accuracy of the glucose data you provide. Incorrect manual entries, stale CGM data, or device malfunctions can lead to inappropriate recommendations. Always verify unusual readings and ensure your CGM device is functioning correctly.

### ⚠️ PRECAUTIONS

**PRECAUTION 1: Glucose Unit Confirmation**
Ensure your glucose unit setting (mg/dL or mmol/L) matches your CGM device and your healthcare provider's records. A mismatch can lead to dangerous misinterpretation of glucose values. Your unit preference is locked during onboarding and can only be changed through a 2-step verification process in Settings.

**PRECAUTION 2: Data Freshness**
Glucose readings are time-sensitive. The app displays the age of each reading ("Last updated: X min ago"). Do not make lifestyle decisions based on readings older than 15 minutes without verifying your current glucose level.

**PRECAUTION 3: CGM Device Compatibility**
This app integrates with specific CGM devices (Dexcom G7, FreeStyle Libre 2/3). Ensure your CGM device is compatible and properly connected. Connection issues may result in missing or delayed data.

**PRECAUTION 4: Network Connectivity**
Some features require internet connectivity. The app maintains limited offline functionality (up to 24 hours of cached data). Ensure regular synchronization for complete functionality.

**PRECAUTION 5: App Updates**
Keep the app updated to the latest version. Updates may include safety improvements, bug fixes, and regulatory compliance updates. Enable automatic updates or check for updates regularly.

**PRECAUTION 6: Account Security**
Protect your account with a strong password or biometric authentication (Face ID / Fingerprint). Do not share your login credentials. Your health data is confidential and protected under applicable data protection laws.

## 7. Residual Risks

Despite comprehensive risk controls, the following residual risks remain when using this device:

| Risk | Description | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Algorithm Limitation** | The app's personalised recommendations are based on population-level data and may not perfectly predict your individual response. | Recommendations include confidence indicators. Always use clinical judgment. |
| **User Misinterpretation** | The app cannot prevent all possible misinterpretations of its outputs by users with varying health literacy levels. | Clear language, visual cues, and persistent disclaimers are provided. |
| **Off-Label Use** | Despite screening measures, some users with Type 1 diabetes or under 18 may access the app. | Contraindications are prominently displayed. Users must confirm eligibility. |
| **Data Quality** | The app relies on user-entered or device-transmitted data that may occasionally be inaccurate or delayed. | Data validation, freshness checks, and error prompts are implemented. |
| **Technology Dependency** | Smartphone failure, battery depletion, or software errors may temporarily interrupt app functionality. | Offline caching and error recovery are implemented. Manual glucose monitoring remains available. |

**Overall Benefit-Risk:** The clinical benefits of enhanced glucose awareness and personalised lifestyle support substantially outweigh these manageable residual risks when the app is used as intended.

## 8. Device Description

### 8.1 What the App Does
[App Name] is a smartphone application that:
1. **Collects glucose data** from manual entries or compatible CGM devices

## 9. Installation and Setup

### 9.1 Installation
1. Download the app from the Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android)
2. Verify the developer name matches: [Manufacturer Name]
3. Install the app on your compatible device

### 9.2 Account Creation
1. Open the app and tap "Get Started"
2. Enter your date of birth (must be 18 or older)
3. Complete the diabetes type questionnaire
4. If you indicate Type 1 diabetes, you will see a warning and be offered "clinician-only" mode or exit
5. Create a secure password or enable biometric login
6. Review and accept the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy

### 9.3 Initial Configuration
1. **Select glucose unit:** Choose mg/dL or mmol/L. This setting is locked and can only be changed with verification.
2. **Set health profile:** Enter height, weight, target weight, activity level, and medications
3. **Connect CGM (optional):** Follow the integration steps for your compatible CGM device
4. **Enable notifications:** Allow notifications for critical glucose alerts and reminders
5. **Complete tutorial:** Watch the mandatory onboarding tutorial explaining app features and limitations

### 9.4 CGM Integration Setup

#### Dexcom G7
1. Ensure you have an active Dexcom G7 account
2. In the app, go to Settings → Connected Devices → Dexcom G7
3. Tap "Connect" and authenticate with your Dexcom credentials
4. Authorise the app to access your glucose data
5. Verify data appears in the app within 5 minutes

#### FreeStyle Libre
1. Ensure you have an active FreeStyle Libre account
2. In the app, go to Settings → Connected Devices → FreeStyle Libre
3. Tap "Connect" and authenticate with your Libre credentials
4. Authorise the app to access your glucose data
5. Verify data appears in the app within 5 minutes

#### Apple HealthKit (iOS)
1. Go to iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → [App Name]
2. Enable "Glucose" read access
3. The app will import historical glucose data from HealthKit

#### Google Health Connect (Android)
1. Install Health Connect from Google Play Store
2. Go to Health Connect → App Permissions → [App Name]
3. Enable "Glucose" read access
4. The app will import historical glucose data from Health Connect

## 10. Operating Instructions

### 10.1 Home Screen
The home screen displays:
- **Current glucose reading** (if synced) with unit, timestamp, and trend arrow
- **Time-in-Range summary** (14-day percentage)
- **Today's insights** (personalised recommendations)
- **Quick actions:** Log glucose, Log weight, View trends

### 10.2 Logging Glucose Manually
1. Tap the "+" button or "Log Glucose"
2. Enter glucose value
3. Select context: Fasting, Before Meal, After Meal (1h), After Meal (2h), Bedtime, Other
4. Add optional notes
5. Tap "Save"

### 10.3 Viewing Trends
1. Tap "Trends" tab
2. Select time period: 1 day, 7 days, 14 days, or 30 days
3. View glucose curve with pattern annotations
4. Tap any data point for details

### 10.4 Viewing Insights
1. Tap "Insights" tab
2. View personalised recommendations based on your recent patterns
3. Each insight shows a confidence level (High, Medium, Low)
4. Tap an insight for detailed explanation and supporting data

### 10.5 Logging Weight
1. Tap "Log Weight" from home screen or Weight tab
2. Enter current weight
3. View weight trend and BMI calculation
4. Correlation with glucose trends displayed automatically

### 10.6 Exporting Data
1. Go to Settings → Data Management → Export Data
2. Select format: CSV, JSON, or PDF Report
3. Select date range
4. Tap "Generate Export"
5. Download link sent to your registered email or available for direct download

### 10.7 Sharing with Healthcare Provider
1. Go to Trends or Insights tab
2. Tap "Share" button
3. Select sharing method: Email, Print, or generate shareable link
4. The shared report includes your glucose trends, insights summary, and weight progress

## 11. Expected Performance

### 11.1 Accuracy Claims
- **Data ingestion:** Glucose values displayed match source data within ±1 mg/dL
- **Trend analysis:** Pattern identification based on established clinical glucose pattern definitions
- **Recommendations:** Generated using evidence-based lifestyle intervention principles

### 11.2 Performance Metrics
- App launch time: <3 seconds
- Glucose data sync: <2 seconds (with good connectivity)
- Insight generation: <5 seconds
- Offline data retention: 24 hours minimum

### 11.3 Limitations
- Recommendations require minimum 3 days of glucose data for initial personalisation
- CGM integration dependent on third-party API availability

## 13. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| **App won't open** | Outdated OS, corrupted install | Update OS; reinstall app; contact support |
| **CGM data not syncing** | API connection issue, expired auth | Re-authenticate CGM in Settings; check CGM device status |
| **Stale data warning** | CGM not transmitting, network issue | Check CGM sensor; verify network connection; enter manually |
| **No insights generated** | Insufficient data (<3 days) | Continue logging glucose; insights appear after 3 days |
| **Incorrect glucose display** | Wrong unit selected | Verify unit in Settings; contact support if unit locked incorrectly |
| **App crashes** | Software bug, memory issue | Force-close and restart; update app; report via Settings |
| **Notifications not received** | Permission denied, Do Not Disturb | Check notification permissions in device Settings; verify DND exceptions |
| **Export fails** | Network issue, large data volume | Retry with smaller date range; ensure stable network |
| **Login issues** | Wrong password, account locked | Reset password; wait 15 minutes if locked; contact support |

## 14. Disposal

This is a software-only device. No physical disposal is required.

To permanently delete the app and your data:
1. Export any data you wish to retain (Section 10.6)
2. Go to Settings → Data Management → Delete Account
3. Confirm deletion (this action is irreversible)
4. Uninstall the app from your device

All data will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days of account deletion, in accordance with GDPR requirements.
