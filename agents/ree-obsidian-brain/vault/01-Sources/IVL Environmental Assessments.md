# IVL Environmental Assessments

## Metadata
- **Authors**: IVL Swedish Environmental Research Institute et al.
- **Type**: Environmental / life-cycle assessment (LCA) studies
- **URL**: https://www.ivl.se/

## Abstract
Environmental and life-cycle assessments of rare earth element mining and processing, quantifying water use, waste generation, and radioactivity from Th/U-bearing minerals such as monazite.

## Key Findings
- REE processing generates radioactive Th/U residues requiring managed disposal
- Water and reagent intensity varies strongly by route (caustic crack vs acid leach)
- LCA exposes hotspots for cleaner process design
