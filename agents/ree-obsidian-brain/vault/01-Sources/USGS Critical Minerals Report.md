## Metadata

## Abstract
The USGS annual critical-minerals assessment documents U.S. and global supply, reserves, and import reliance for rare earth elements and other critical minerals, informing policy and risk analysis.

## Key Findings
- Tracks REE mine production, reserves, and net import reliance
- Highlights concentration of processing in China
- Informs the U.S. critical-minerals list and strategy
