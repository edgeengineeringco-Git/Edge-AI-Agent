# MOC — Deposits Index

## Primary REE Deposit Types

### Carbonatite-Hosted (LREE Dominant)
- [[Carbonatite Deposits]] — Bayan Obo, Mountain Pass, Araxá
- Primary minerals: bastnäsite, monazite
- TREO: 3–9% | HREO:TREO: 1–5%

### Ion-Adsorption Clay (HREE Dominant)
- [[Ion-Adsorption Clay Deposits]] — Jiangxi, Longnan, Myanmar
- Adsorbed ions on kaolinite/halloysite — no discrete REE minerals
- TREO: 300–3000 ppm | HREO:TREO: 25–45%

### Hydrothermal HREE
- [[Hydrothermal HREE Deposits]] — Browns Range, Nolans Bore, Dubbo
- Primary minerals: xenotime, fluorapatite
- TREO: 0.3–3% | HREO:TREO: 30–75%

### Placer / Heavy Mineral Sands
- [[Placer Monazite Deposits]] — Chavara, Manavalakurichi
- Primary mineral: monazite
- HMC monazite: 50–200 kg/t

## Pathfinder Elements by Type
- **Carbonatite**: Th, Nb, Ba, Sr, P, F, Zr
- **Alkaline**: Zr, Nb, Ta, Hf, Ga, F
- **IAC Clay**: CIA >80, low magnetic susceptibility
- **Hydrothermal**: F, P, Ca, Ba, Sr
