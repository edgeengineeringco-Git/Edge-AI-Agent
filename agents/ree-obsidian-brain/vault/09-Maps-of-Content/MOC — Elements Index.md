# MOC — Elements Index

## Light REE (LREE)
- [[Lanthanum]] — La (Z=57) — Catalysts, battery alloys
- [[Cerium]] — Ce (Z=58) — Polishing, catalysts, glass
- [[Praseodymium]] — Pr (Z=59) — NdFeB magnets (with Nd)
- [[Neodymium]] — Nd (Z=60) — **Primary magnet metal**
- [[Promethium]] — Pm (Z=61) — Man-made only, no stable isotopes
- [[Samarium]] — Sm (Z=62) — SmCo magnets, neutron absorbers
- [[Europium]] — Eu (Z=63) — Phosphors, red color in displays

## Middle REE (MREE)
- [[Gadolinium]] — Gd (Z=64) — MRI contrast, reactor control

## Heavy REE (HREE)
- [[Terbium]] — Tb (Z=65) — **Magnet coercivity at high temp**
- [[Dysprosium]] — Dy (Z=66) — **Critical for EV motors**
- [[Holmium]] — Ho (Z=67) — Specialized magnets, lasers
- [[Erbium]] — Er (Z=68) — Fiber optics, lasers
- [[Thulium]] — Tm (Z=69) — Medical imaging (X-ray)
- [[Ytterbium]] — Yb (Z=70) — Fiber lasers, alloy strengthening
- [[Lutetium]] — Lu (Z=71) — PET scan detectors, catalysts

## REE-like
- [[Scandium]] — Sc (Z=21) — Al-Sc alloys (aerospace), SOFCs
- [[Yttrium]] — Y (Z=39) — Phosphors, YSZ ceramics, garnets
