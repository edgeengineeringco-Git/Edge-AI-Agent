# MOC — Processing Index

## Beneficiation & Concentration
- [[Bastnäsite Flotation]] — Carbonatite concentrate route
- [[Monazite Gravity Separation]] — Placer and some carbonatites
- [[Xenotime Recovery]] — Hydrothermal HREE deposits
- [[In-Situ Leaching]] — IAC clay deposits

## Leaching Technologies
| Ore Type | Lixiviant | Conditions | Recovery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bastnäsite | H₂SO₄ 6 M, 90°C | Roast-acid | 85–95% |
| Monazite | NaOH 2 M, 300°C | Caustic crack | 85–98% |
| Monazite | HCl 6 M, 90°C | Acid leach | 90–98% |
| Eudialyte | HCl 6 M, 70°C | Silica gel risk | 60–75% |
| IAC Clay | (NH₄)₂SO₄ 0.5 M, 70–90°C | In-situ/heap | 50–80% |

## Separation & Purification
- [[Solvent Extraction]] — Industrial standard (98–99% recovery)
- [[Ion Exchange]] — Magnet-grade Nd, Pr (95–99%)
- [[Selective Precipitation]] — Bulk HREE separation
- [[Molten Salt Electrolysis]] — Nd, Dy, Tb metal production

## Refining
- [[Oxalate Precipitation]] → calcine → tech-grade REO (98–99.9%)
- [[Re-leach + Re-precipitate]] → 4N oxide (99.99%)
- [[Arc Melting]] → NdFe, DyFe master alloys
