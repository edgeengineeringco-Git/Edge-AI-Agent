# Analytical Methods

## Overview
**Analytical methods** for REE determination include ICP-MS (low-detection multi-element), XRF/pXRF (rapid screening), INAA, and gamma spectrometry. Method choice balances detection limits, throughput, and cost.

## Comparison
| Method | Strength | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| ICP-MS | ppb multi-element | lab-based, dissolution |
| pXRF | field speed | limited REE sensitivity |
| Gamma spec | Th/U, K | no direct REE |
