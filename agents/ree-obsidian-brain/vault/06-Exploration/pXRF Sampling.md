# pXRF Sampling

## Overview
**pXRF sampling** uses portable XRF for rapid in-field screening of REE pathfinders (Zr, Nb, Y, Th, U) and triage of samples before lab ICP-MS.

## Use
- Real-time grade triage at outcrop / core
- Pathfinder mapping (Zr–Nb–Y–Th)
- Reduces lab load
