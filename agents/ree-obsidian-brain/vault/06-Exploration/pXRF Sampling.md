# pXRF Sampling

Portable X-ray fluorescence analysis of rock/channel samples in the field for REE pathfinders (Th, Nb, Zr, Y, Rb, Sr, Ba, Pb). Enables real-time targeting of carbonatite and alkaline complexes before lab dispatch.
