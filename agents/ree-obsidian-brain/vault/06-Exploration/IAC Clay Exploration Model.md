# IAC Clay Exploration Model

## Overview
The **ion-adsorption clay (IAC) exploration model** targets weathered granitic profiles where REE³⁺ are adsorbed onto clays, recoverable by [[In-Situ Leaching]].

## Criteria
- Residual soils on REE-rich granites
- HREE enrichment in upper profile
- High leachable (ammonium-extractable) REE
- Low total but high HREO %
