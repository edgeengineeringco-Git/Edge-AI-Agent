# Soil Geochemistry

## Overview
**Soil (and gully) geochemistry** detects REE/pathfinder dispersion halos over buried or masked REE mineralization — a low-cost reconnaissance tool.

## Pathfinders
- REE, Y, Zr, Nb, Th, U
- Anomalies in transported overburden
