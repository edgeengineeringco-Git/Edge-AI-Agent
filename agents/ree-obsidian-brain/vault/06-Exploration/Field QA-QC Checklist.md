# Field QA-QC Checklist

## Overview
A **field QA/QC checklist** ensures defensible REE geochemical datasets: duplicate and blank samples, certified reference materials (CRMs), and strict chain-of-custody.

## Checklist
- [ ] Field duplicates (≥5% of samples)
- [ ] Blank inserts (contamination check)
- [ ] CRM recovery within tolerance
- [ ] Chain-of-custody log
- [ ] Consistent sample prep ([[Sample Preparation]])
