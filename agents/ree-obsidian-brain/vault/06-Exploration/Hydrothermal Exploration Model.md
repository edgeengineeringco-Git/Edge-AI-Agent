# Hydrothermal Exploration Model

## Overview
The **hydrothermal HREE exploration model** targets xenotime/apatite-REE systems in veins, breccias, and alteration zones fed by F-rich, HREE-bearing fluids.

## Criteria
- Veins/breccias, intense alteration (sericite, fluorite)
- HREE + Y, Nb, F, Zr pathfinders
- High Y/Ho, negative–positive Eu* signatures
