# Drone Surveys

## Overview
**Drone surveys** mount magnetometers, gamma spectrometers, and hyperspectral sensors on UAVs for rapid, high-resolution REE target mapping over rugged or vegetated terrain.

## Payloads
- Magnetometer → [[Magnetic Susceptibility]]
- Gamma spectrometry → [[Gamma-Ray Spectrometry]]
- Hyperspectral → alteration/mineral mapping
