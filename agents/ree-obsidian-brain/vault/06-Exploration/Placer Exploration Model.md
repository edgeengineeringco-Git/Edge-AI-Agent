# Placer Exploration Model

## Overview
The **placer exploration model** targets heavy-mineral sands concentrating dense REE minerals (monazite, xenotime, zircon, ilmenite) in beach, dune, and paleochannel environments.

## Criteria
- High-density mineral concentration
- Modern beaches / reworked dunes / paleochannels
- Co-product Ti, Zr, REE
