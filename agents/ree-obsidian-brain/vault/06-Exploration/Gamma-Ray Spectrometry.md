# Gamma-Ray Spectrometry

## Overview
**Gamma-ray spectrometry** measures natural K, Th, and U from the air or ground. High Th and elevated **Th/U ratios** flag REE-bearing minerals (monazite, xenotime, thorite).

## Use
- Map thorium anomalies over carbonatite/placer
- Th/U ratio as REE proxy
- Tie to magnetic surveys
