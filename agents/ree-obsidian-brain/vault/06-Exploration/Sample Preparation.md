# Sample Preparation

## Overview
**Sample preparation** — crushing, pulverizing, and riffle-splitting to <75 µm — produces a representative subsample for REE geochemical analysis. Poor prep is the top source of error.

## Steps
1. Crush to <2 mm
2. Pulverize to <75 µm
3. Riffle-split for analysis
4. Retain pulp duplicate
