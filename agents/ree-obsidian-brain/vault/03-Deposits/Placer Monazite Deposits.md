# Placer Monazite Deposits

## Overview
Placer monazite deposits form from the weathering and concentration of heavy minerals in beach and alluvial environments. Monazite [(Ce,La,Nd,Th)PO₄] is a dense (5.2 g/cm³), durable phosphate that concentrates with other heavy minerals (ilmenite, rutile, zircon). These deposits are significant REE sources, particularly in India and Brazil.

## Characteristics
| Parameter | Range |
|---|---|
| Monazite in HMC | 50–200 kg/t heavy mineral concentrate |
| TREO in monazite | 55–65% (very high in concentrate) |
| HREO:TREO | 5–15% (LREE-dominant) |
| Key Minerals | Monazite, xenotime (minor) |
| Associated | Ilmenite, rutile, zircon, leucoxene |

## Geochemical Pathfinders
- **Radiometric**: Th signature (monazite is Th-bearing)
- **Heavy mineral concentrate**: Monazite identifiable by orange-pink colour
- **Beach ridge systems**: Quaternary raised beaches

## Major Examples
| Deposit | Country | HMC Monazite | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chavara | India | ~2% of HMC | Producing (Ti by-product) |

| Buena | Brazil | Variable | Historic |
| Green Cove Springs | USA | Historic | Closed |

## Processing
- **Beneficiation**: Gravity + magnetic + electrostatic separation
- **Monazite concentrate**: 85–95% purity
- **Recovery**: 60–80%
- **Challenge**: Th radioactivity (NORM regulations)

## Radioactivity Note
Monazite concentrates >200 Bq/g are classified as radioactive source material. Processing requires:
- Radiation worker protocols
- Th-bearing residue disposal
- Caustic crack generates Th-rich waste

## Exploration Criteria
1. Ancient or modern beach strandlines
2. Source terrain with REE-enriched bedrock (granite, gneiss)
3. Active longshore drift systems
4. Quaternary sea-level highstands (raised beaches)

## Strengths & Weaknesses
| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Very high concentrate grade | Th radioactivity |
| Simple gravity processing | NORM regulatory burden |
| Often Ti/Zr by-product credit | Small resource size |
| Established in India/Brazil | Low HREO content |
