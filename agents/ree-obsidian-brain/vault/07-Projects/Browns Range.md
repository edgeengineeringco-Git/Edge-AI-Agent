# Browns Range

## Overview
Browns Range is Australia's most advanced heavy rare earth project and the Western world's best hope for non-Chinese dysprosium and terbium supply. It is the archetype for hydrothermal HREE deposits, with xenotime as the primary ore mineral. The project has operated a pilot plant since 2018 but full commercial production has faced financing challenges.

## Location
- **Region**: Tanami Desert, Northern Territory, Australia
- **Nearest Town**: Halls Creek, WA (~220 km)
- **Access**: Gravel road (seasonal), 30 km from Tanami Road
- **Climate**: Arid; hot summers, mild winters

## Ownership
- **Developer**: Northern Minerals Limited (ASX: NTU)
- **Key Investors**: Iluka Resources (strategic), various Australian super funds
- **Offtake**: MoU with multiple international parties

## Mineralogy
- **Xenotime**: Dense (4.4–5.1 g/cm³), yellow-brown to reddish, high relief
- **Grain size**: 10–200 μm (fine, requiring fine grinding)
- **Gangue**: Quartz, K-feldspar, muscovite, biotite, fluorite
- **Alteration**: Greisen, sodic-calcic, potassic

## Processing Route
- **Crushing**: To P80 ~150 μm (fine grind required)
- **Beneficiation**: Gravity (xenotime is dense) + magnetic separation
- **Concentrate**: ~20% TREO (xenotime-rich)
- **Leaching**: Caustic crack (NaOH) or acid bake
- **Separation**: SX circuit for Dy/Tb/Y separation
- **Recovery**: ~65–75% (lower than carbonatite due to fine grain size)

## Strategic Significance
- **Western HREE supply**: Only advanced non-Chinese Dy/Tb source
- **Australian critical minerals**: Federal + NT government priority project
- **Defence applications**: Potential US/Australian defence offtake
- **Downstream**: Discussions with magnet manufacturers for integrated supply

## Strengths & Challenges
| Strengths | Challenges |
|---|---|
| Highest HREO:TREO globally | Low grade (0.3–1.0% TREO) |
| Non-Chinese source | Complex mineralogy |
| Political support (Aus/US) | Fine-grained xenotime (hard to recover) |
| Premium basket price | High CAPEX for low grade |
| Pilot plant de-risks processing | Financing in difficult REE market |

## Exploration Upside
- **Gardner Range**: 15 km north, similar geology, limited drilling
- **Dazzler**: High-grade zone (1.5% TREO) within Browns Range
- **Area play**: Tanami region has multiple xenotime prospects
