# Baotou

## Overview
**Baotou** is the giant industrial complex in Inner Mongolia that processes [[Bayan Obo]] REE concentrates — the largest REE separation and refining hub in the world, central to [[China REE Supply]].

## Key Facts
- Processes Bayan Obo carbonatite concentrates
- World's largest separation capacity
- Associated environmental/radioactivity challenges
