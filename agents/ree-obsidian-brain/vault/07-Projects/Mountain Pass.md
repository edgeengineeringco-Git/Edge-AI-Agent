# Mountain Pass

## Overview
Mountain Pass is the only operating rare earth mine in the United States and one of the highest-grade carbonatite REE deposits in the world. It was the global leader in REE production from the 1960s until the 1990s when Chinese production undercut prices. MP Materials acquired the mine in 2017 and brought it back into production.

## Location
- **State**: California, USA
- **County**: San Bernardino
- **Geology**: Mojave Desert, Clark Mountain Range
- **Elevation**: ~1,400 m

## Ownership
- **Current**: MP Materials (NYSE: MP)
- **Previous**: Molycorp (bankrupt 2015)

## Processing
- **Crushing/Grinding**: On-site to <150 μm
- **Flotation**: Selective bastnäsite flotation
- **Concentrate**: ~60% REO
- **Leaching**: HCl leach of concentrate
- **Separation**: Partial separation on-site; some concentrate shipped to China for full separation (being brought on-shore)

## Status Timeline
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1949 | Discovered by uranium prospectors |
| 1952 | Production begins (Molycorp) |
| 1965–1995 | World's leading REE producer |
| 2002 | Environmental issues (pipe leak) halt operations |
| 2012 | Molycorp reopens with $1.25B investment |
| 2015 | Molycorp bankruptcy; mine idled |
| 2017 | MP Materials acquires mine |
| 2020 | Back in production; concentrate export to China |
| 2022 | Separation facility construction begins |
| 2024 | On-site separation operational |

## Strategic Significance
- **US supply security**: Only domestic REE mine
- **Defense critical**: Key Nd/Pr source for US military magnets
- **Downstream integration**: Building full separation + magnet manufacturing
- **DoD contracts**: Multiple awards for heavy rare earth processing R&D

## Strengths & Challenges
| Strengths | Challenges |
|---|---|
| Very high grade (7–9% TREO) | Very low HREO content |
| Large resource base | No Dy, Tb, Eu production |
| Established infrastructure | Californian environmental regulations |
| US geopolitical priority | Historical environmental legacy |
| Integrated downstream plans | Still relies on China for some separation |
