# Ytterbium (Yb)

## Overview
Ytterbium is unusual among REEs for having a stable +2 oxidation state (like Eu). Yb²⁺ is formed under strongly reducing conditions and substitutes for Ca²⁺. Yb is widely used in fiber lasers, atomic clocks, and as a doping agent in steelmaking.

## Economic Significance
- **Fiber lasers**: Yb-doped fiber lasers — industrial cutting/welding
- **Atomic clocks**: Yb optical lattice clocks (most precise timekeepers)
- **Steelmaking**: Grain refiner in stainless steels
- **Price**: ~$80/kg (8× La baseline, high volatility)
- **Criticality**: ★★★☆☆ — some substitutes in lasers

## Exploration Notes
- Yb can show anomalies in strongly reducing environments (rare)
- Yb/Lu ratio is often the most stable HREE pair for normalization
- Enriched in IAC clays relative to carbonatites
