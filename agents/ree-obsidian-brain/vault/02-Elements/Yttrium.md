# Yttrium (Y)

## Overview
Yttrium is the archetypal REE-like element — it is trivalent with an ionic radius between Ho and Er, causing it to co-occur with HREE in virtually all deposits. Y is not a lanthanide (Group 3, period 5) but is universally grouped with REEs in exploration, mining, and markets.

## Economic Significance
- **YSZ ceramics**: Yttria-stabilized zirconia — thermal barrier coatings, oxygen sensors
- **Phosphors**: Red phosphor (Y₂O₂S:Eu³⁺) in displays
- **Magnets**: Y is sometimes added to NdFeB for grain boundary control
- **Price**: ~$60/kg (6× La baseline, medium volatility)
- **Criticality**: ★★★☆☆ — moderate substitutes in most applications

## Occurrence
- Co-occurs with HREE in all deposit types
- IAC clays: Y is a significant component of HREO
- Xenotime (YPO₄) is the primary Y mineral
- Carbonatites: Y is depleted relative to HREE

## Exploration Notes
- Y is always reported with HREE in resource estimates
- Y/Ho ratio is a powerful geochemical proxy (chondritic = ~28)
- Y anomaly (Y/Y*) can indicate marine vs. terrestrial REE signatures
