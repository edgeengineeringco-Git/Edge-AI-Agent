# Neodymium (Nd)

## Overview
Neodymium is the single most important REE for the global energy transition. It is the primary constituent of NdFeB permanent magnets, which power EV motors, wind turbines, and consumer electronics. Nd demand is growing at ~12–15% CAGR driven by electrification.

## Economic Significance
- **Primary magnet metal**: 30–32 wt% of sintered NdFeB magnets
- **EV motors**: 0.32–0.96 kg Nd per vehicle
- **Wind turbines**: 54–402 kg Nd per turbine (direct-drive)
- **Price**: ~$85/kg (very high volatility)
- **Criticality**: ★★★★★ — highest supply-chain risk

## Exploration Notes
- Co-varies strongly with Pr (ρ > 0.8)
- LREE-dominant deposits (carbonatite) are primary Nd sources
- Nd is not redox-sensitive — behaves as pure trivalent
