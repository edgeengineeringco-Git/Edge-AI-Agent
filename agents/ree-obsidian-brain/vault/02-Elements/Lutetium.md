# Lutetium (Lu)

## Overview
Lutetium is the heaviest and smallest lanthanide. It is the last element of the series and shows the strongest lanthanide contraction effect. Lu is extremely scarce (0.4 ppm) and the most expensive naturally occurring REE due to its specialized applications in PET scanners and catalysts.

## Economic Significance
- **PET scanners**: Lu-based scintillators (LYSO, LuAG) — **no perfect substitute**
- **Catalysts**: Petroleum cracking catalysts
- **Price**: ~$550/kg (55× La baseline, very high volatility)
- **Criticality**: ★★★★☆ — functionally irreplaceable in PET

## Exploration Notes
- Lu is the anchor for HREE normalization (chondrite values)
- Lu/Hf ratio is fundamental for geochronology (Lu-Hf dating)
- Almost never economically targeted individually
