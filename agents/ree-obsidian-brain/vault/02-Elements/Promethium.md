# Promethium (Pm)

## Overview
Promethium is unique among the REEs: it has **no stable isotopes** and does not occur naturally in mineable concentrations. All promethium is produced artificially in nuclear reactors. It sits between neodymium and samarium in the periodic table but is never encountered in exploration or mining.
