# Holmium (Ho)

## Overview
Holmium has the highest magnetic moment per unit volume of any known element. It is used in specialized high-field magnets, laser crystals, and nuclear control rods. While less commercially prominent than Dy or Tb, Ho is strategically important for advanced magnet and medical applications.

## Economic Significance
- **Specialized magnets**: Ho can partially substitute for Dy in niche applications
- **Lasers**: Ho:YAG lasers for medical and military use
- **Nuclear**: Ho-165 has large neutron cross-section
- **Price**: ~$200/kg (20× La baseline, high volatility)
- **Criticality**: ★★★★☆ — some substitutability exists

## Exploration Notes
- Ho/Dy ratio is relatively constant in most deposits
- Enriched in IAC clays and some alkaline complexes
- Magnetic susceptibility surveys can detect Ho at high concentrations
