# Erbium (Er)

## Overview
Erbium is best known for its role in fiber-optic telecommunications. Erbium-doped fiber amplifiers (EDFAs) revolutionized long-distance optical communication by directly amplifying 1550 nm signals without electronic conversion. Er is also used in lasers and nuclear applications.

## Economic Significance
- **Fiber optics**: EDFAs are the backbone of global internet infrastructure
- **Lasers**: Er:YAG medical and dental lasers
- **Metallurgy**: Er improves workability of vanadium alloys
- **Price**: ~$50/kg (5× La baseline, medium volatility)
- **Criticality**: ★★★☆☆ — moderate substitutability in some apps

## Exploration Notes

- Er/Yb ratio useful for fingerprinting deposit types
