# Thulium (Tm)

## Overview
Thulium is the second-rarest naturally occurring REE (only Lu is comparable at 0.4 ppm). It has no known biological role and very specialized applications in medical imaging and portable X-ray devices. Tm-170 is a useful radioisotope produced in reactors.
