# Lanthanum (La)

## Overview
Lanthanum is the first element of the lanthanide series and the namesake of the group. It is the most abundant LREE after cerium and neodymium. La is diamagnetic (no unpaired 4f electrons) and chemically behaves as a prototype trivalent REE.

## Economic Significance
- **Catalysts**: FCC catalysts in petroleum refining (largest use)
- **Battery alloys**: NiMH battery cathodes (LaNi₅Hₓ)
- **Glass**: high-refractive-index optical glass
- **Price**: ~$10/kg (baseline, low volatility)

## Exploration Notes
- La/Ce ratio useful for identifying carbonatite vs. alkaline affinities
- Enriched in bastnäsite and monazite
- No redox anomaly — purely trivalent
