# Gadolinium (Gd)

## Overview
Gadolinium sits at the boundary between Middle and Heavy REE. It is the only REE with a Curie temperature above room temperature (293 K), making it ferromagnetic at ambient conditions. Gd is critical for MRI contrast agents and high-temperature magnet grades.

## Economic Significance
- **MRI contrast**: Gd-DTPA is the standard contrast agent
- **Magnets**: Added to NdFeB for high-temperature grades
- **Nuclear**: Gd-157 has highest thermal neutron capture cross-section
- **Price**: ~$120/kg (12× La baseline, high volatility)
- **Criticality**: ★★★★☆ — substitutable in some MRI applications

## Exploration Notes
- Gd marks the MREE/HREE transition in normalized patterns
- Strong magnetic susceptibility — detectable by magnetic surveys
- Price correlation with Tb (ρ > 0.8)
