# Praseodymium (Pr)

## Overview
Praseodymium is a critical magnet REE used alongside neodymium in NdFeB permanent magnets (as NdPr alloy). It is the third most critical REE for the energy transition after Nd and Dy. Pr has a distinctive green colour in its salts.

## Economic Significance
- **Permanent magnets**: NdFeB magnets use NdPr alloy (typically 25–30% Pr)
- **Glass**: yellow-green colouring, UV-cut goggles
- **Price**: ~$80/kg (8× La baseline, high volatility)
- **Criticality**: ★★★★★ — functionally irreplaceable in magnets

## Exploration Notes
- Co-varies strongly with Nd (ρ > 0.8)
- Pr/Nd ratio is relatively constant in most deposits (~0.25–0.30)
- Anomalous Pr/Nd may indicate unusual fractionation or alteration
