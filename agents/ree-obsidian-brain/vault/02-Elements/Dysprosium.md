# Dysprosium (Dy)

## Overview
Dysprosium is the **critical enabler of EV motors**. Without Dy, NdFeB magnets lose coercivity at temperatures above 80°C. Dy is added to automotive-grade magnets to maintain performance at 150–200°C. It is the second-most supply-constrained REE after terbium.

## Economic Significance
- **EV motors**: 0.04–0.30 kg Dy per vehicle
- **Wind turbines**: Direct-drive generators use Dy-bearing grades
- **Price**: ~$300/kg (30× La baseline, very high volatility)
- **Criticality**: ★★★★★ — no scalable substitute in high-T magnets
- **Demand growth**: +300–400% by 2030

## Dy Scaling in Magnets
| Operating Temp | Dy Content | Cost Impact |

## Exploration Notes
- Dy is the defining HREE for project economics
- HREO:TREO ratio is the key metric; >25% is premium
- IAC clays (China, Myanmar, Vietnam) are primary sources
- Browns Range-type hydrothermal deposits can reach 55–75% HREO
