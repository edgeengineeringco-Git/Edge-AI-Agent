# Scandium (Sc)

## Overview
Scandium is chemically REE-like (trivalent, similar ionic radius to Lu) but geologically distinct. It is not a lanthanide — it sits in Group 3 above Y and La. Sc is the lightest REE-like element and has the highest strength-to-weight ratio of any aluminum alloy additive.

## Economic Significance
- **Al-Sc alloys**: Aerospace components, sporting goods (highest specific strength)
- **SOFCs**: Scandia-stabilized zirconia solid oxide fuel cells
- **Lighting**: Metal-halide lamps (sunlight-spectrum)
- **Price**: ~$150/kg (15× La baseline, high volatility)
- **Criticality**: ★★★★☆ — growing aerospace demand

## Occurrence
- Lateritic deposits over ultramafic rocks (Ni-Co laterite by-product)
- Uranium mill tailings (by-product)
- Titanium and tungsten ores (trace)
- **Primary Sc deposits are extremely rare**

## Exploration Notes
- pXRF cannot reliably detect Sc (low Z, light element)
- ICP-MS or LIBS required for Sc analysis
- Ni-Co laterite projects may have significant Sc credit
