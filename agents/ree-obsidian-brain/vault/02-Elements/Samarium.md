# Samarium (Sm)

## Overview
Samarium marks the transition from Light REE to Middle REE. It is the key constituent of SmCo permanent magnets, which outperform NdFeB at very high temperatures (>300°C). Sm also has important nuclear applications as a neutron absorber.

## Economic Significance
- **SmCo magnets**: Niche but critical for aerospace and military (high T_max)
- **Nuclear**: Sm-149 is a strong neutron poison; used in control rods
- **Price**: ~$25/kg (moderate volatility)
- **Criticality**: ★★★☆☆ — substitutable by Nd in most applications

## Exploration Notes
- Sm/Gd ratio helps distinguish carbonatite from alkaline affinities
- Enriched in monazite relative to bastnäsite
- Magnetic susceptibility surveys can detect Sm at sufficient concentration
