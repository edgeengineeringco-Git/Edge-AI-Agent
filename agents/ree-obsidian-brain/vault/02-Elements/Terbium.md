# Terbium (Tb)

## Overview
Terbium is one of the scarcest and most valuable HREE. It is essential for high-temperature NdFeB magnets where it boosts coercivity even more effectively than dysprosium. Tb has the highest ferromagnetic moment of any REE (9.72 μ_B) and is irreplaceable in premium magnet grades.

## Economic Significance
- **Magnet coercivity**: Premium EV motors use 2–5% Tb
- **Phosphors**: Green phosphor in displays
- **Price**: ~$850/kg (85× La baseline, extreme volatility)
- **Criticality**: ★★★★★ — minimal substitutes, highest supply risk
- **Supply**: 80% China production concentration

## Magnet Demand
| Grade | Tb Content | Application |
|---|---|---|
| N48SH-UH | 2% | High-performance EV |
| 200°C+ grades | 6–10% + Tb | Aerospace, military |

## Exploration Notes
- Tb is enriched in IAC clays and some hydrothermal deposits
- Carbonatites are poor Tb sources (HREO:TREO < 5%)
- Strong price correlation with Dy (ρ > 0.8)
