# Arc Melting

## Overview
**Arc melting** (vacuum/consumable-electrode) produces rare-earth permanent-magnet alloy ingots (NdFeB, SmCo) and rare-metal master alloys from separated REE metals. It controls composition and minimizes oxidation.

## Steps
1. Reduce REE chloride/fluoride (see [[Molten Salt Electrolysis]])
2. Vacuum arc-melt with Fe, B, Co
3. Strip-cast / crush / mill to powder
