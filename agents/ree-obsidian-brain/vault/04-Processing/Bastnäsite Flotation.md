# Bastnäsite Flotation

Froth flotation of bastnäsite [(Ce,La)CO₃F] from carbonatite ores using fatty-acid collectors at pH ~8–9. Produces a REE concentrate (30–70% TREO) after magnetic separation of associated barite and calcite. Primary upgrade route at Bayan Obo and Mountain Pass.
