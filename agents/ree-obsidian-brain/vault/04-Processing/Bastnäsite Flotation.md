# Bastnäsite Flotation

## Overview
**Bastnäsite flotation** is the primary beneficiation step for carbonatite REE ores, using fatty-acid or hydroxamate collectors to float bastnäsite away from calcite/barite/dolomite.

## Key Facts
- Collectors: hydroxamates, fatty acids, phosphates
- Depressants: silicates, gangue carbonates
- Produces LREE concentrate → [[Solvent Extraction]]
