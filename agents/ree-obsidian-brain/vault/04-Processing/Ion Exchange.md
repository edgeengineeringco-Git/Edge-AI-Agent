# Ion Exchange

## Overview
**Ion exchange** uses selective resins to adsorb and differentially elute individual REEs, especially valuable HREEs that are hard to separate by solvent extraction alone.

## Use
- Final purification of individual oxides
- HREE separation (Y, Dy, Er, Yb)
- Often paired with [[Solvent Extraction]]
