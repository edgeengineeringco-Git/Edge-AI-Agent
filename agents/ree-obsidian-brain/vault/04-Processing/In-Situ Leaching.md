# In-Situ Leaching

## Overview
**In-situ leaching (ISL)** of ion-adsorption clays percolates a salt solution (electrolyte, (NH₄)₂SO₄, or NaCl) through the weathered profile to desorb exchangeable REE³⁺ cations, avoiding conventional mining.

## Key Facts
- Recovers adsorbed REE, not lattice-bound
- Low capital, low waste rock
- Ammonium/salt regulations are key constraint
