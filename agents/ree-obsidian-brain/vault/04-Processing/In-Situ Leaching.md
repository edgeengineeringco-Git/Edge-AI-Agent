# In-Situ Leaching

Leaching of ion-adsorption clay (IAC) deposits with (NH₄)₂SO₄ or electrolyte in place — no blasting or tailings dam. Recovers adsorbed REE³⁺ ions directly from the exchange sites on kaolinite/halloysite clays at 50–80% REO.
