# Xenotime Recovery

Recovery of xenotime (YPO₄), the primary HREE host, from hydrothermal and placer streams. Typically via gravity + magnetic separation, then caustic or acid digestion to recover Y, Dy, Tb, Er, Yb at HREO:TREO up to 60–75%.
