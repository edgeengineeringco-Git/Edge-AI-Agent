# Xenotime Recovery

## Overview
**Xenotime recovery** extracts HREE-rich xenotime (YPO₄) from placer and heavy-mineral streams. It is a primary source of Y, Dy, Er, and Yb.

## Key Facts
- Xenotime: YPO₄, HREE-dominant
- Recovered with monazite in placer circuits
- High value due to Dy/Tb/Y content
