# Solvent Extraction

Industrial standard for REE separation. Sequential mixer-settlers with organophosphorus extractants (e.g. PC88A, D2EHPA) split the lanthanide series by ionic radius, achieving 98–99.9% individual REO purity. Driven by lanthanide contraction.
