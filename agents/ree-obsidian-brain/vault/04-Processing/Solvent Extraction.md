# Solvent Extraction

## Overview
**Solvent extraction (SX)** is the industrial workhorse for multi-element REE separation, using counter-current mixers/settlers with organophosphorus extractants (P507/HEH(EHP), P204) to isolate individual REEs at scale.

## Key Facts
- Hundreds of stages for full separation
- Extractants: P507, P204, Cyanex 272
- Feed: leach liquor from ore/concentrate
