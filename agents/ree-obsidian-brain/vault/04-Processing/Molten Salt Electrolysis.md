# Molten Salt Electrolysis

## Overview
**Molten salt electrolysis** reduces REE chlorides/fluorides in a molten salt bath to produce metallic Nd, Pr, Dy, and Tb for magnet alloying.

## Key Facts
- Bath: REE chloride + alkali chloride flux
- Products: REE metals for [[Arc Melting]]
- High energy, strict anhydrous control
