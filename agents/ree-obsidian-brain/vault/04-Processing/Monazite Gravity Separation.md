# Monazite Gravity Separation

Concentration of heavy monazite [(Ce,La,Nd,Th)PO₄] from placer and carbonatite feeds using spiral concentrators, shaking tables, and magnetic separators. Monazite's high density (~5.2 g/cm³) and paramagnetism enable clean separation from gangue.
