# TREO Calculation

Total Rare Earth Oxide = sum of the individual REO equivalents (RE₂O₃) of La₂O₃, CeO₂, Pr₆O₁₁, Nd₂O₃, Sm₂O₃, Eu₂O₃, Gd₂O₃, Tb₄O₇, Dy₂O₃, Ho₂O₃, Er₂O₃, Tm₂O₃, Yb₂O₃, Lu₂O₃ and Y₂O₃. Primary mass-grade metric for REE ores.
