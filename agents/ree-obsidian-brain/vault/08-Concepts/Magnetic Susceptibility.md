# Magnetic Susceptibility

## Overview
**Magnetic susceptibility** is the induced magnetization per unit applied field. Carbonatites and alkaline complexes often carry magnetite/pyrochlore, giving strong magnetic contrast used in airborne and ground geophysics to map REE prospective bodies.

## Use in REE exploration
- Delineate circular carbonatite plugs
- Map fenitization aureoles
- Tie to gravity/radiometric surveys
