# Laterite Deposits

## Overview
**Laterites** are intensely weathered residual ores forming in tropical humid climates. Best known for Ni–Co, some laterite profiles also carry ion-adsorption-style REE enrichment and are a notable **scandium** source (e.g., New Caledonia, Queensland).

## Key Facts
- Formed by intense tropical weathering
- Host Ni–Co–REE–Sc in oxidized profile
- REE leachable in ion-adsorption layers
