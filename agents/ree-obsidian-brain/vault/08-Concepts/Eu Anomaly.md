# Eu Anomaly

## Overview
A **Eu anomaly** is the deviation of Eu from the chondrite-normalized Sm–Gd trend. Because Eu²⁺ substitutes into plagioclase feldspar while other REE are trivalent, feldspar fractionation drives a **negative Eu\*** (europium depletion) in evolved rocks.

## Formula
> Eu* = Eu_N / √(Sm_N × Gd_N)

## Interpretation
- **Negative Eu* (Eu-star)** → plagioclase fractionation / felsic differentiation
- **Positive Eu* (Eu-star)** → plagioclase accumulation or reducing mafic melts
