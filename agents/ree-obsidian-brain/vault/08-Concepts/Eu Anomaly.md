# Eu Anomaly

Deviation of Eu from the smooth REE pattern, expressed as Eu/Eu* = Euₙ/√(Smₙ·Gdₙ). Negative (Eu depletion) signals feldspar fractionation in magmatic systems; a key petrogenetic and alteration indicator in REE exploration.
