# Eu Anomaly

Europium anomaly (usually negative) arising from fractionation of Eu²⁺ into feldspar during plagioclase crystallisation in felsic magmas. A negative Eu anomaly is a hallmark of granitic/ evolved sources; positive Eu anomalies signal plagioclase accumulation. Key feldspar-fractionation proxy.
