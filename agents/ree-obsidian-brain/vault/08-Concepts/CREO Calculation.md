# CREO Calculation

CREO (Critical Rare Earth Oxide) — the sum of critical REE oxides (typically Nd2O3 + Pr6O11 + Dy2O3 + Tb4O7 + Eu2O3) within TREO, used for criticality-weighted grade assessment.
