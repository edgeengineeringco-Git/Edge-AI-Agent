# CREO Calculation

**CREO (Critical Rare Earth Oxide)** is the sum of oxide equivalents of the strategically critical "magnet" REE — Neodymium, Praseodymium, Dysprosium, Terbium, and Europium. It focuses grade reporting on the elements that drive supply-chain risk.

## Formula
> CREO = Nd₂O₃ + Pr₆O₁₁ + Dy₂O₃ + Tb₄O₇ + Eu₂O₃

## Why it matters
- Highlights the constrained, high-value fraction of TREO
- Aligns with defence/clean-energy criticality assessments
- Often <15% of TREO but dominates economic value
