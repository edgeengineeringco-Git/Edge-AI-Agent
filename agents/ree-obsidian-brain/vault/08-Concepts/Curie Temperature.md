# Curie Temperature

## Overview
The **Curie temperature (Tc)** is the temperature above which a ferromagnet loses its spontaneous magnetization and becomes paramagnetic. It sets the upper service-temperature limit for rare-earth permanent magnets.

## Values
| Magnet | Tc (°C) |
|---|---|
| NdFeB | ~310 |
| SmCo (Sm₂Co₁₇) | ~720 |
| SmCo₅ | ~720 |

## Relevance
High-Tc SmCo magnets suit aerospace/defense; NdFeB needs Dy/Tb doping to retain coercivity near its lower Tc.
