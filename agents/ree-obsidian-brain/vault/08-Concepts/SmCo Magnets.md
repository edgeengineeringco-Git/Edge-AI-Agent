# SmCo Magnets

## Overview
**SmCo (samarium–cobalt)** magnets offer the best high-temperature and corrosion performance of commercial permanent magnets (Tc ≈ 720 °C). They are used in aerospace, defense, and high-heat motor applications where NdFeB would demagnetize.

## Key Facts
| Phase | Approx. Tc |
|---|---|
| SmCo₅ | ~720 °C |
| Sm₂Co₁₇ | ~720 °C |
