# Cut-Off Grades

## Overview
A **cut-off grade** is the minimum ore grade at which a unit of material is economically worth mining and processing. REE cut-offs vary strongly by deposit type because of differing TREO, HREO content, and processing cost.

## Typical REE cut-offs (illustrative)
| Deposit Type | TREO cut-off | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Carbonatite | ~1.0–1.5% TREO | LREE-rich, low cost |
| Ion-Adsorption Clay | ~300–500 ppm TREO | HREE-rich, leachable |
| Placer Monazite | ~0.5–2% heavy mineral | gravity recoverable |
| Hydrothermal HREE | ~0.3–1% TREO + Y | high value HREE |
