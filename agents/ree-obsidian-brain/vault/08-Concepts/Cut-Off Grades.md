# Cut-Off Grades

Minimum TREO (or CREO) grade at which rock is economic to mine. Typical cut-offs: carbonatite 1–2% TREO, IAC clay 300–500 ppm TREO, hydrothermal 0.3–0.6% TREO, placer 50–100 kg/t monazite. Drives tonnage and contained-metal estimates.
