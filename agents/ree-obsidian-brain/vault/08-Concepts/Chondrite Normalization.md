# Chondrite Normalization

## Overview
**Chondrite normalization** divides measured REE concentrations by those of a primitive chondritic meteorite (CI or PAAS for sedimentary). This flattens the ~3-order-of-magnitude abundance trend, revealing enrichment, depletion, and anomalies.

## Standard Values (CI chondrite, ppm)
| La | Ce | Pr | Nd | Sm | Eu | Gd | Tb | Dy | Ho | Er | Tm | Yb | Lu |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.237 | 0.613 | 0.0927 | 0.457 | 0.149 | 0.0567 | 0.199 | 0.0357 | 0.246 | 0.0549 | 0.161 | 0.0247 | 0.161 | 0.0247 |

## Use
- Reveal **Eu\*** and **Ce\*** anomalies
- Compare deposit-type REE patterns
- Identify carbonatite vs. IAC vs. hydrothermal signatures
