# Aluminum Alloys

## Overview
Scandium (and to a lesser extent Er, Y) is added to aluminium alloys to refine grain structure and improve strength, weldability, and corrosion resistance. **Al–Sc** and **Al–Sc–Zr** alloys are used in aerospace and sporting goods.

## Key Facts
- Optimal Sc addition: ~0.1–0.5% Sc
- Al₃Sc dispersoids pin grain boundaries
- Sc is the highest-value REE use per kg
