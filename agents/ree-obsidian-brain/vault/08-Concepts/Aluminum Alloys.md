# Aluminum Alloys

Scandium is added to aluminium alloys (typically 0.1–0.5% Sc) to form fine Al₃Sc precipitates that refine grain structure, raise strength, weldability, and fatigue/corrosion resistance. The dominant commercial Sc end-use — aerospace, defence, and high-performance sporting goods.
