# Eudialyte

Complex zircono-silicate (Na₁₅Ca₆(Fe,MMn)₃Zr₃Si₂₆O₇₂(OH,Cl)₂) hosting REE up to several %. An emerging alkaline-rock resource; HCl leaching gives 60–75% REO recovery with a silica-gel challenge.
