# Eudialyte

Sodium zirconosilicate (Na₁₅Ca₆(Fe,Mn)₃Zr₃Si₂₆O₇₂(OH,Cl)₂) hosting disseminated REE, an alkaline/agpaitic-complex REE mineral. Attractive as a low-Th alternative REE source; HCl leaching is challenged by silica-gel formation.
