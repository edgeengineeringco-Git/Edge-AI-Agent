# Y/Ho Ratio

Yttrium mimics HREE chemistry but is not a lanthanide. A near-chondritic Y/Ho (~28) indicates co-genetic REE behaviour, whereas elevated or depressed ratios fingerprint marine vs. terrestrial, hydrothermal, or supergene processes.
