# Y/Ho Ratio

## Overview
The **Y/Ho ratio** is a powerful geochemical fingerprint. Chondrites and most crustal rocks have Y/Ho ≈ **28** (Y and Ho are nearly inseparable chemically), so deviations signal specific processes.

## Interpretation
| Setting | Y/Ho | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Chondrite / mantle | ~28 | primitive |
| Hydrothermal / seawater | >>28 | Y mobility, Ho retention |
| Carbonatite | often elevated | fractionated fluids |
