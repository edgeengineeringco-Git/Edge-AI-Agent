# Ce Anomaly

Positive or negative cerium anomaly in a shale-normalised REE pattern. Ce³⁺ oxidises to insoluble Ce⁴⁺ under oxic marine conditions, producing a negative Ce anomaly; reducing/ hydrothermal fluids can give a positive Ce anomaly. A primary redox / paleoredox indicator in REE pattern interpretation.
