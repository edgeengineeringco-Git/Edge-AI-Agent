# Ce Anomaly

Deviation of Ce from the smooth REE pattern, expressed as Ce/Ce* = Ceₙ/√(Laₙ·Prₙ). Positive in oxidising marine settings, negative under reducing conditions — a redox proxy in exploration and sedimentology.
