# Ce Anomaly

## Overview
A **Ce anomaly** is the deviation of Ce from the smooth chondrite-normalized REE pattern defined by La and Pr. Under oxic conditions Ce³⁺ oxidizes to insoluble Ce⁴⁺ and is removed, producing a **negative** anomaly; reducing/suboxic settings can give a **positive** anomaly.

## Formula
> Ce* = Ce_N / √(La_N × Pr_N)
Values < 0.9 (negative) or > 1.1 (positive) are anomalously fractionated.

## Interpretation
- **Negative Ce* (Ce-star)** → oxic weathering / marine Fe–Mn crusts
- **Positive Ce* (Ce-star)** → anoxic/suboxic reducing settings
