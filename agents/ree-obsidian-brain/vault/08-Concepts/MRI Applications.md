# MRI Applications

## Overview
**Gadolinium** chelates (e.g., Gd-DTPA / gadopentetate) are the dominant **T1 MRI contrast agents**, shortening relaxation times to enhance soft-tissue imaging. Gd's high magnetic moment (7 unpaired f-electrons) makes it ideal.

## Key Facts
- Gd³⁺ is paramagnetic, safely sequestered by chelation
- Largest non-magnet specialty Gd market
- Macrocyclic chelates preferred for stability
