# Radioactivity in REE Deposits

## Overview
REE ores frequently co-carry **Th and U** (notably in monazite, xenotime, and uraniferous minerals). This radioactivity drives regulatory classification, worker safety, and radioactive waste handling in REE processing.

## Key numbers
- Monazite concentrate: ~100–500 Bq/g
- >200 Bq/g = regulated radioactive source material (license required)
- ThO₂ in monazite: 4–12%

## Implications
- Caustic-crack / acid-leach residues are often radioactive waste
- Thorium by-product is both a liability and a potential fuel resource
