# HREO Calculation

Heavy Rare Earth Oxide = sum of Gd₂O₃ through Lu₂O₃ plus Y₂O₃. The HREO:TREO ratio (1–5% carbonatite → 30–75% hydrothermal/IAC) is the dominant value driver.
