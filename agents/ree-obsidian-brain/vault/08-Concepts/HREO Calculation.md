# HREO Calculation

## Overview
**HREO (Heavy Rare Earth Oxide)** is the sum of heavy rare earth oxide equivalents — Gd₂O₃ through Lu₂O₃ plus Y₂O₃ (yttrium behaves as an HREE geochemically).

## Formula
> HREO = Gd₂O₃ + Tb₄O₇ + Dy₂O₃ + Ho₂O₃ + Er₂O₃ + Tm₂O₃ + Yb₂O₃ + Lu₂O₃ + Y₂O₃

## Why it matters
HREO (especially Dy, Tb, Y) commands a large price premium over LREE and is the constrained segment of the market.
