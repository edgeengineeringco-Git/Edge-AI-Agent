# Lanthanide Contraction

## Overview
The **lanthanide contraction** is the steady decrease in trivalent ionic radius from La³⁺ (1.032 Å) to Lu³⁺ (0.861 Å) across the series, caused by poor shielding of the 4f electrons. It controls mineral site preference and LREE/HREE fractionation.

## Consequences
- HREE fit smaller, higher-charge sites (xenotime, zircon)
- LREE favor larger sites (carbonate, bastnäsite, monazite)
- Explains why Y³⁺ (~0.90 Å) geochemically mimics Ho–Er
