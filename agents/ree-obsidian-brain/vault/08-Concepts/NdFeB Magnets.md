# NdFeB Magnets

## Overview
**NdFeB (neodymium–iron–boron)** magnets have the highest energy product of any permanent magnet (BHmax ≈ 35–52 MGOe). They dominate EV traction motors, wind generators, and hard-drive/spindle applications, consuming the bulk of Nd/Pr plus Dy/Tb for high-temperature coercivity.

## Key Facts
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Composition | Nd₂Fe₁₄B (+ Dy/Tb) |
| Tc | ~310 °C |
| Market | ~44% of REO demand |
