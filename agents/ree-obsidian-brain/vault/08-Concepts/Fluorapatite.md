# Fluorapatite

Ca₅(PO₄)₃F, the dominant REE host in many hydrothermal and carbonatite deposits (e.g. Nolans Bore). Substitutes REE³⁺ for Ca²⁺, often with Na; a key mineralogical control on HREE endowment.
