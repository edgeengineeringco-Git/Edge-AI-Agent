# Fiber Lasers

## Overview
**Rare-earth-doped fiber lasers** use Yb³⁺ (high-power kW-class materials processing) and Er³⁺ (1.55 µm telecom) as gain media. They deliver excellent beam quality and thermal management.

## Key Facts
- Yb-doped fiber: 1.0–1.1 µm, very high power
- Er-doped fiber: 1.55 µm, telecom/amplifier
