# Australia REE Resources

## Overview
Australia hosts nationally significant REE endowment across carbonatites ([[Mount Weld]], OLADE, Mordor), ion-adsorption clays in weathered granites (NSW, QLD), and heavy-mineral sands (Eneabba, Murray Basin). See Jaireth et al. (2014).
