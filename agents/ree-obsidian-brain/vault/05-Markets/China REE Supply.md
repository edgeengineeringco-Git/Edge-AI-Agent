# China REE Supply

## Overview
**China** dominates global REE production (~60–70% of REO) across Bayan Obo carbonatite and southern ion-adsorption clays, and controls the majority of separation capacity. Export controls and quotas strongly shape global markets.

## Key facts
- Bayan Obo: world's largest REE resource ([[Bayan Obo]], [[Baotou]])
- Southern IAC clays: HREE-rich
- Dominant separator/refiner
