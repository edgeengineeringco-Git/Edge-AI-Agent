## Market Highlights

- **China dominance continues**: 68% of global REE mining, 89% of processing and separation capacity
- **Myanmar supply disruption**: Political instability affecting in-country mining operations, ~12% of global light REE supply at risk
- **MP Materials expansion**: Mountain Pass now processing separated NdPr on-site, targeting 6,000 tpa by Q4 2026
- **Lynas rare earths**: Kalgoorlie processing plant fully operational, Mt Weld expansion to 12,000 tpa TREO underway
- **Ion-adsorption clay projects advancing**: Brazilian and African laterite REE projects attracting significant investment

## Demand Drivers

- **Wind turbines**: 2-3 tonnes NdPr/MW for direct-drive permanent magnet turbines — China installed 85 GW in 2025
- **EV traction motors**: ~1.5 kg NdPr per typical BEV — global EV sales reached 18M units in 2025
- **Defence**: F-35, guided missiles, naval propulsion — strategic stockpiling by US, EU, Japan
- **Electronics**: Miniature magnets in smartphones, hard drives, speakers, and AR/VR headsets
