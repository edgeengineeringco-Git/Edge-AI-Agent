# Permanent Magnet Demand

Aggregate NdFeB/SmCo magnet demand — ~44% of total REO use (68 kt/yr, 2025). Spans EVs, wind, robotics, and HDDs; the metric that sets the floor for LREE and magnet-HREE prices.
