# Global REE Production 2025

## Overview
World rare earth oxide (REO) production in **2025** was led by China, with significant output from the USA (Mountain Pass), Myanmar, and Australia (Lynas). Total REO ≈ 350–400 kt.

## Producers (illustrative 2025)
| Country | t/yr REO | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| China | ~250–270k | carbonatite + IAC |
| USA | ~12k | [[Mountain Pass]] |
| Myanmar | ~30–40k | IAC (HREE) |
| Australia | ~20–25k | [[Mount Weld]] / Lynas |
