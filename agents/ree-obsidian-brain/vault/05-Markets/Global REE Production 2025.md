# Global REE Production 2025

2025 output ~151 kt REO/yr: China ~60%, USA (Mountain Pass) ~12 kt, Malaysia (Lynas) ~12 kt, Australia/MISC remainder. HREO share skewed to IAC clay and Lynas; carbonatite dominates LREE tonnage.
