# REE Demand Forecast 2025-2035

## Overview
Base-case REE demand grows from ~151 kt REO (2025) to ~229 kt REO (2035), with persistent HREE (Dy, Tb) shortfalls of 22–45 kt. Driven by magnets (EV + wind) and electronics.

## Scenario (REO)
| Year | Demand | HREE % | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 | 151 kt | 23% | — |
| 2030 | 188 kt | 29% | 22–28 kt |
| 2035 | 229 kt | 31% | 35–45 kt |
