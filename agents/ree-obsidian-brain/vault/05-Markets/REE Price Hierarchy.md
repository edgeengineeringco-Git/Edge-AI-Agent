# REE Price Hierarchy

## Overview
**REE price hierarchy** reflects scarcity and demand: abundant LREE (La, Ce) are cheap, magnet LREE (Nd, Pr) mid-range, and HREE (Dy, Tb, Tm, Lu) command large premiums.

## Approximate USD/kg (illustrative)
| Tier | Elements | ~USD/kg |
|---|---|---|
| Abundant | La, Ce | <10 |
| Magnet LREE | Nd, Pr | ~80–85 |
| Magnet HREE | Dy, Tb | ~300–850 |
| Scarce HREE | Tm, Lu | >1,000 |
