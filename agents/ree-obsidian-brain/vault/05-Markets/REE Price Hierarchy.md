# REE Price Hierarchy

REE oxides span a ~80× price range: La/Ce ~$2–3/kg to Tb ~$850/kg and Tm/Lu ~$300–800/kg. Driven by crustal scarcity, separation cost, and magnet/HREE strategic demand (see Master Index quick table).
