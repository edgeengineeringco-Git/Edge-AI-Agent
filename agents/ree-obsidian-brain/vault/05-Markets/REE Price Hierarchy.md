# REE Price Hierarchy

Relative price ordering of REOs: HREE (Tb, Dy, Eu, Y) and magnet LREE (Nd, Pr) command the highest USD/kg, while La and Ce are near byproduct give-away prices. Drives beneficiation economics and co-product credits.
