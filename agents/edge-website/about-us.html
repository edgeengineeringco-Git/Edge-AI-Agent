Our Story

Science-Driven. Impact-Focused.

Earthwise Dynamics Geo Environs (EDGE) is a research-focused team of engineers and scientists delivering advanced solutions in geotechnical engineering, environmental assessment, and geospatial analysis.

Based in Ireland, we collaborate with leading higher education institutions like Trinity College Dublin, UCD, and DIAS, and have access to environmental laboratories across Europe. With strong connections to instrumentation providers in the UK and mainland Europe, we utilise state-of-the-art sensors, drones, and analytical tools to support both research and commercial projects.

Our commitment is to deliver precise, data-driven results that drive sustainable outcomes and support resilient communities. Our approach is distinct: we integrate AI, big data processing, and advanced geospatial methods to deliver precise, impactful results.
