6. Policy, Regulation and Strategic Context

EU — Critical Raw Materials Act (CRMA) 2026 Updates

RESourceEU Action Plan (Dec 2025 — Active 2026): The European Commission has adopted the RESourceEU Action Plan, mobilising ~€3 billion for CRM projects over 12 months. This includes the establishment of a European Critical Raw Materials Centre (modelled on Japan's JOGMEC) to coordinate joint purchasing, stockpiling, and market intelligence. Export restrictions on permanent magnet scrap and aluminium scrap are being implemented in Q2 2026, with lithium-ion battery waste export bans to non-OECD countries effective September 2026.

  • Jurisdiction: European Union
  • URL: Mining Journal — €3B EU spend ↗

EU Council Position on CRMA Amendments (4 March 2026): The EU Council has adopted a negotiating position that centralises oversight at the European Commission level. Large companies (>500 employees, >€150M turnover) using strategic raw materials face new obligations: supply chain risk assessments, diversification reporting, and potential single-supplier limits. Digital product passports will be required for compliance on permanent magnets. The amendments are expected to be adopted by mid-2027.

  • Jurisdiction: European Union
  • URL: EU Council Position ↗

2030 Strategic Targets (Unchanged)

  • 10% of EU annual consumption to be extracted domestically
  • 40% to be processed within the EU
  • 25% to come from recycling
  • Max 65% of any strategic raw material from a single third country

Geopolitical Context

Chinese export restrictions on rare earths, gallium, germanium, and graphite continue to drive EU policy. The EU now treats CRMs as an economic security issue. Restrictions on Chinese entities participating in EU-funded CRM R&I are being implemented. The European Critical Raw Materials Centre will counterbalance Chinese dominance. These dynamics create a favourable policy environment for EU-based CRM exploration technology providers like EDGE.

  • Jurisdiction: Global
  • URL: Chambers — RESourceEU Analysis ↗
