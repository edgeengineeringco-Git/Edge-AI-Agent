Thematic Observation

The critical minerals landscape is entering a new phase: from policy announcements to execution risk. Western governments have deployed the financial tools ($120B Vault, €40B US project support, $7.5B Russia, C$3.6B Canada), but the 15+ year lead times for mines and 5–10 year timelines for new processing capacity mean the supply-demand tension will persist through the 2030s. The real action is shifting to who controls the midstream — processing, separation, and refining — not who owns the deposits.
