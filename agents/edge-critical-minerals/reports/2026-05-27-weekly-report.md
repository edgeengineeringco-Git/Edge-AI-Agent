## 1. Market Movements

### Lithium
- **GFEX LC2606 futures**: ¥168,960/t (down 14% from May 13 peak of ¥205,260/t). **Spot battery-grade**: ~¥179,700/t.
- **Warehouse warrants accumulating**: ~132,000 t on GFEX (+28%), contributing to bearish sentiment.
- **Supply-restart wave**: Mt Holland expansion approved (4.4M t/yr, 2× current); Mt Marion FID ($490M AUD for flotation plant + underground mine); Bald Hill restart after 18 months; Core Lithium Finniss restarting; SEMCORP terminated $2B Malaysia separator project.
- **Bullish supply factors still active**: Zimbabwe export quotas (no ore until late July); Jiangxi clay mine license renewals (7,500 t/yr LCE at risk); Greenbushes FY2026 cut by ~20kt LCE; Chile exports -5.1% MoM.
- **Demand**: April battery output 183.9 GWh (+55.6% YoY); May +10.5% MoM. ESS now 31% of lithium demand.
- **Analyst divergence**: CITIC ¥250k/t in 2–3 months; UBS ¥298k/t. Market consensus shifting toward near-term pullback then medium-term bullish.

### Rare Earth Elements
- **China's expanded controls (12 REEs)**: Suspended until November 10, 2026 — legal architecture intact. Extraterritorial "50% penetration rule" applies to foreign entities using Chinese-origin materials.
- **Separation bottleneck**: China controls ~90% of global separation capacity, ~93% of magnet production. Non-China buildout faces 5–10 year timelines.
- **DARPA "SMASH" Program**: 48-month, two-phase program to recover all ~80 stable elements from ore with near-zero waste.
- **Ucore RapidSX**: $4M DoD demonstration + $18M Louisiana facility. Column-based separation ~3× faster.
- **Nth Cycle + Ionic Rare Earths**: Electro-extraction to replace oxalic acid (sourced from China) in REE refining. Integration at Belfast facility from Q4 2026.
- **Price bifurcation**: Ex-China processed REEs command 4–6× Chinese domestic prices. NdPr elevated on US defense stockpiling.

### Cobalt & Nickel
- **Cobalt (LME)**: $56,290/t (flat w/w). DRC 96,600 t/yr export quota (~55% cut) continues to drive structural deficit of 36,000–91,000 t projected for 2026.
- **Nickel (LME)**: $18,560/t (flat w/w). Indonesia mining quotas cut ~30% (379M → 260–270M wmt). Eramet Weda Bay remains offline.
- **Sulfuric acid crisis**: China banned H₂SO₄ exports (May 1), threatening HPAL nickel processing costs in Indonesia.
- **Battery chemistry**: LFP now ~81% of China power battery installations. High-nickel NCM remains essential for premium EVs.

### Copper
- **LME Copper**: $13,624/t (down $44 w/w). Near record highs.
- **Deficit estimates**: JPMorgan 330kt, UBS >400kt, ICSG ~150kt for 2026.
- **Demand**: AI data centres ~475kt in 2026 (+110kt YoY). EVs use 3–4× copper vs ICE. Grid modernisation accelerating globally. S&P Global projects 50% demand increase by 2040 (28M → 42M tonnes).
- **Supply**: Ore grades declining below 0.6%. New mines take ~17 years from discovery to production. Grasberg force majeure, Kamoa-Kakula guidance slashed. TC/RC 2026 annual benchmark at $0/t; spot at negative $45–70/t.

### Graphite & Battery Minerals
- **Natural graphite**: Flake prices depressed (~$800–1,200/t). Supply growth (966kt → 1.6Mt) outpacing demand.
- **Synthetic graphite**: Prices trending modestly upward (~$2,500–4,000/t) on tight capacity and rising coke costs.
- **China control**: ~90% of global anode production, ~98% of graphitization capacity.
- **US anti-dumping**: 93.5% on Chinese anode-grade graphite (July 2025) — procurement patterns not yet shifted.
- **Northern Graphite**: Moved majority of spot sales to Metalshub (March 2026) — signal of non-Chinese price discovery.
- **Sodium-ion**: CATL targeting 40 GWh capacity by end-2026; could reduce graphite demand intensity at the margin.

## 2. Geopolitical Developments

### US–China Rivalry
- **FORGE Initiative**: 54 countries + EU, succeeding Minerals Security Partnership. Korea first rotating chair.
- **Project Vault**: $120B strategic stockpile. Minimum pricing/tariff walls under discussion by USTR.
- **21 bilateral CRM frameworks** signed since Trump's return; 17 more under negotiation.
- **MP Materials**: $400M DoW investment + $150M loan for heavy REE separation at Mountain Pass. 10-year NdPr floor of $110/kg.
- **China's 15th Five-Year Plan** expected to prioritise strategic materials self-sufficiency, reducing technology transfer.

### Resource Nationalism Wave
- **Synchronised restrictions**: DRC (cobalt quotas), Indonesia (nickel cuts), Zimbabwe (lithium quotas), Namibia (raw mineral export ban + 51% local ownership proposal), China (REE controls).
- **OECD**: Export restrictions on critical minerals increased ~5× between 2009–2024.
- **"Third pole" emerging**: African leadership aligning with Middle Eastern capital (Saudi Arabia, UAE) to build processing capabilities outside US-China binary.
- **G20 Critical Minerals Framework** (2025 Johannesburg) formally endorsed "beneficiation at source."

### Russia & Central Asia
- Russia consolidating Central Asia (Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan) as secure resource hinterland for uranium, copper, rare metals.
- India cut Russian crude imports by ~800,000 bpd since Oct 2025 under US pressure.
- Three spheres of influence: China in Africa, US in Americas/Greenland, Russia in Central Asia — Europe structurally outside all three.

## 5. Technology Watch

### Recycling & Processing Breakthroughs
- **INL EC-Leach (March 2026)**: >95% Li, Co, Ni, Mn recovery from batteries using electricity. 75% less energy, 88–90% less chemicals.
- **Nth Cycle electro-extraction**: Replaces Chinese-sourced oxalic acid in REE refining. Eliminates hidden chemical supply chain dependency.
- **Supra Elemental Recovery (UT Austin)**: 3D-printed reusable molecular-sponge cartridges for Co, Li, Ga, Sc from tailings/e-waste.
- **Altilium EcoMineral™**: Up to 70% Cu leaching from 110M-tonne tailings using biodegradable lixiviants. Near-neutral pH, ambient pressure.
- **DARPA SMART Program** (May 2026): University of Utah test bed for biotechnologies to extract REEs from phosphate mine waste, acid mine drainage, and e-waste. First users: DARPA's EMBER program (microbial REE separation).

### Substitution
- **Al-for-Cu debate**: ~70% of 2023 global copper consumption replaceable by aluminium. Barrier is entrenched reputation.
- **Sodium-ion batteries**: CATL scaling to 40 GWh by end-2026. Hard-carbon anodes eliminate graphite.
- **Solid-state batteries**: Compatible with ternary (NCM) chemistry — sustaining Co/Ni demand.

### Exploration Tech
- **Silex (UK)**: Modular micro-refineries for REE recovery from industrial waste streams.
- **Supercritical fluid extraction (WashU)**: scCO₂/scN₂ processes achieving ~100% purity REE from coal fly ash.
- **Magnetic separation from granite waste (Italy)**: Recovery of REEs, Y, Sc, Li from extractive waste.

### Price Forecast Signals
- **Lithium**: Near-term correction from supply-restart news. Medium-term bullish (¥190–210k floor). CITIC/UBS ¥250–298k vs Goldman sees H1 peak.
- **Cobalt**: Strongly bullish — structural deficit entrenched. Limited substitution options near-term.
- **Nickel**: Moderately bullish — policy cuts offset by high LME inventories (~250kt).
- **Copper**: Structurally bullish — deficit consensus 150–400kt. AI/electrification creating unprecedented demand. New supply 5–10 years away.
- **REE**: Ex-China premium (4–6×) to persist until Western separation comes online (late 2020s).
- **Graphite**: Near-term oversupply masks long-term structural tightness. Ex-China demand growing 14% annually.

### Geopolitical Flashpoints
1. **Nov 10, 2026** — China REE control suspension expiry (most consequential single catalyst for heavy REEs)
2. **Synchronised resource nationalism cluster** — DRC + Indonesia + Zimbabwe + China + Namibia
3. **US–China mineral trade war escalation** — Project Vault + potential July tariffs
4. **Strait of Hormuz / Iran** — Cascading sulfuric acid crisis into copper production
5. **EU funding gap** — US deployed 8× EU CRM funding. Europe risks falling behind in supply security

### Thematic Observation
The critical minerals landscape is shifting from **policy announcements to execution risk**. Western governments have deployed massive financial tools ($120B Vault, €40B+ US project support, C$3.6B Canada, A$1.4B Australia) but the 15+ year lead times for new mines and 5–10 year timelines for processing capacity mean supply-demand tension persists through the 2030s. The real action is moving to the **midstream** — the race to build non-Chinese processing, separation, and refining capacity.
