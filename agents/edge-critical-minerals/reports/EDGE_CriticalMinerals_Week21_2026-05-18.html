6. Policy, Regulation and Strategic Context

  • EU – RESourceEU Action Plan (Dec 2025): The European Commission adopted the RESourceEU Action Plan accelerating CRM strategy delivery. Key 2026 measures include: European Critical Raw Materials Centre (early 2026), export restrictions on permanent magnet scrap and waste lithium-ion batteries (from Sep 2026), €3B mobilised for CRM projects (€2B EIB + €300M Battery Booster + €600M Horizon Europe), and mandatory recycled content shares for permanent magnets. FDI screening tightened for strategic projects. URL: Chambers analysis ↗
  • EU – CRMA Labelling Extension (Early 2026): Extended labelling requirements for products containing permanent magnets (hard disk drives, speakers, drones, motorized toys). Mandatory recycled content shares for permanent magnets from pre- and post-consumer waste recycled inside the EU. URL: Eversheds CRMA guide ↗
  • US – Critical Minerals as National Security Priority (Jan 2026): US Treasury convened allied finance ministerial (Australia, Canada, EU, Japan, South Korea, India, UK) focused on derisking critical minerals supply chains. Theme: "Derisking rather than decoupling." $1.6B federal funding to USA Rare Earth (Round Top, Texas), ~$500M to NioCorp (Elk Creek, Nebraska). USGS 2026 report shows US now 100% import-reliant on 16 critical minerals. URL: US Treasury ministerial ↗
  • US – Defense Production Act for Mining: New financing template: government offtake + cost-plus contracts + streamlined DPA permitting. Exemplified by Ares Strategic Mining's Lost Sheep fluorspar mine restart with Pentagon offtake. Average US mine timeline: 29 years — structural bottleneck. URL: Skillings mining trends ↗
  • Canada – DoD Funding for REE Separation: Ucore Rare Metals received $22.4M DoD grant for heavy rare earth (Sm, Gd) domestic refining using RapidSX separation tech. DoD mandates exclude restricted-source magnets from 2027 onward. URL: US-Canada rare earth supply chain ↗
  • EU – China Restrictions on R&I Funding: Restrictions on Chinese entities participating in EU-funded CRM research and innovation being extended to other EU funding streams. Foreign direct investment screening tightened for strategic CRM projects. URL: IEU Monitoring RESourceEU ↗
