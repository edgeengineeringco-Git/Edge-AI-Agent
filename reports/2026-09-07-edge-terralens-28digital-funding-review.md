## REVIEWER 1 — Deep-Tech & Digital Innovation (28DIGITAL mandate)
**Lens:** Is this genuinely digital/deep-tech with defensible technical novelty? TRL, IP, AI/EO fusion.

**Strengths**
- Squarely digital + deep-tech: multi-sensor fusion (drone gamma + LiDAR, satellite hyperspectral, geochemistry, SEM mineralogy, geological records) into one REE prospectivity engine.
- Explicit **uncertainty quantification, explainability and reproducibility** (model registry, documented API) — rare and exactly what responsible-AI deployment in geoscience needs.
- Credible maturity claim: "validated, commercially deployable," tested on REE prospectivity; ESA BIC Ireland backing de-risks execution.

**Weaknesses / Concerns**
- **No validation evidence** — no accuracy/recall/ROC vs. known deposits, although the brief explicitly scores outputs "against ground truth." Claimed TRL unsubstantiated in the deck.
- "Identify rare-earth targets today and orbital/lunar tomorrow" reads as ambition, not capability; geochemical inference from orbit remains speculative.
- No disclosure of the fusion algorithm, training-data provenance, or representativeness of test sites.

**Score: 7.0 / 10 — Fund.** Strong fit with 28DIGITAL's deep-tech mandate; proof-gap is the main risk.

## REVIEWER 2 — Venture & Scale-up / Investment (5% SAFE lens)
**Lens:** 28DIGITAL takes 5% SAFE equity, so investability, market size, scalability and team matter as much as tech.

**Strengths**
- Market timing is exceptional: the EU Critical Raw Materials Act (2030: 10% extraction / 40% processing domestic) makes REE discovery a first-order EU political/economic priority; buyers (geological surveys, explorers/miners, agencies) are funded and under mandate.
- Multiple revenue lines sketched (platform licensing, subscription, site services, multi-sensor products, "intelligence atlas," future space instrument).
- Stated traction: LOIs, Enterprise Ireland / ESA support, Maynooth University & RISE pilot partners, "Industry Connect."

**Weaknesses / Concerns**
- **Business model is vague** — no pricing, SAM/SOM, or quantified willingness-to-pay; unclear whether surveys (cash-constrained) or miners (cash-rich) are the anchor customer.
- **No traction numbers** — LOIs ≠ revenue; no pilot-to-contract conversion.
- **Monetising public-data outputs** — if the engine mostly adds value to *public* survey data, the IP/capture model must be defended.
- **Equity/deal:** 5% SAFE is standard for 28DIGITAL and acceptable, but the deck is silent on cap table, prior dilution, and whether the SAFE terms are founder-friendly at this stage.

**Score: 6.0 / 10 — Conditional Fund.** Right market; the venture-case proof a venture builder would demand is missing.

## REVIEWER 3 — Co-Creation & Industry-Partner Fit
**Lens:** 28DIGITAL's model is startups + industry partners co-developing real challenges. Does the consortium and pilot plan satisfy the brief's partner roles?

**Strengths**
- Logical pilot flow: **Align & Prepare → Configure & Integrate → Model & Review → Demonstrate**, with KPIs (European pilot site, prospectivity TPU, validated signal).
- Acknowledges the brief's success criteria (expert-reviewed zone/signal; "honest outputs" auditable by R&D geologists; documented recalibration).
- Names credible pilot partners (RISE, Maynooth University).

**Weaknesses / Concerns**
- **Consortium not fully constituted** — only pilot partners named; no prime/coordinator clarity and no confirmed R&D geological reviewer with the brief's defined "Training-Delivery Leader" role; no letters of commitment in the package.
- KPI framing is asserted, not evidenced (no ground-truth results, no audit trail).
- Risk register / schedule / milestones referenced by the brief are not presented.

**Score: 6.5 / 10 — Conditional Fund.** Good challenge alignment; partner governance must be completed.

## REVIEWER 4 — EU Digital Agenda & Programme Compliance (ex-EIT Digital heritage)
**Lens:** Strategic EU digital-sovereignty / talent / market-creation fit, eligibility, and proposal discipline.

**Strengths**
- Exceptional EU relevance: REE/CRM discovery directly serves European strategic autonomy and the digital/clean-tech supply chain — core to the EIT Digital successor mission.
- Genuine space/EO asset use (satellite hyperspectral, drone gamma/LiDAR, future instrument) — real space-added-value, not "space-washing."
- Builds on a prior ESA engagement (continuity 28DIGITAL values in staged incubation).

**Weaknesses / Concerns**
- **Proposal hygiene is poor** — submission folder contains a "Test 1" *duplicate* of the call, three unreadable `mso*.tmp` Office temp files, and a 70 MB video with no index/transcript. For a venture builder that judges founder discipline, this signals weak proposal process and risks ineligible/unclear deliverables.
- **No budget / co-funding / resource plan** — EC-style or 28DIGITAL-stage inputs, eligible-cost treatment, and the SAFE interaction are all absent.
- Success-criteria mapping asserted not evidenced; no risk register.

**Score: 5.5 / 10 — Do not fund *as submitted*; invite a complete proposal.** Concept is on-mission; the package is incomplete and undisciplined.

### 🟡 PANEL VERDICT: **CONDITIONAL FUND — advance to the next 28DIGITAL stage (interview / MVP build), contingent on a complete, evidenced submission.**

**Why this fits 28DIGITAL's process:** 28DIGITAL runs a *staged, venture-building* model (two-stage incubation, MVP development, mentorship, 5% SAFE). A high-strategic-fit, deep-tech, EU-sovereignty-aligned applicant like EDGE is exactly its target — but the material submitted is a **pitch deck, not a fundable stage-1 dossier**. The gaps are in *completeness and proof*, not in the concept, which is why a conditional advance (rather than outright reject or outright award) matches the programme's gate-based design.

**Conditions precedent to progression:**
1. **Validation dossier** — blind ground-truth test vs. known REE deposits; quantitative accuracy/uncertainty metrics (the brief's core scoring axis).
2. **Constituted consortium** — confirmed partners in the brief's defined roles + letters of support; named R&D geological reviewer.
3. **Venture & deal clarity** — pricing/SAM-SOM, ≥1 paying/committed pilot, cap-table & SAFE-term clarity (the 5% SAFE is acceptable and standard, but terms should be confirmed founder-friendly).
4. **Resource & milestone plan** — budget/inputs, risk register, schedule, KPI baseline for the pilot.
5. **Proposal hygiene** — replace the deck with a structured submission; remove temp/test artefacts; provide an indexed/video summary or transcript.

**Equity note:** The 5% SAFE is consistent with 28DIGITAL's standard terms and not a barrier; panel advises confirming valuation cap and pro-rata/post-money mechanics before signing.

**If conditions cannot be met, the panel would recommend NO FUND and encourage resubmission in a later cohort.** The strategic and technical merits justify continuing the relationship; the current package does not yet justify an unconditional award.
